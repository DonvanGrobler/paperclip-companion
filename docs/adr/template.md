## Context

What requirement, constraint or observed problem needs a decision? Link the relevant plan section, issue and evidence.

## Alternatives

Describe the feasible choices, including keeping the existing approach. Record concrete tradeoffs rather than implying the chosen option is already approved.

## Proposed decision

State the narrow change, affected modules and exclusions. Mark provisional choices clearly. For runtime/dependency decisions, record exact versions and official sources checked on the review date.

## Data, permission and license effects

Identify any new network destination, storage, credential handling, capture trigger, IPC operation, OS permission or third-party source/asset. Explain the denial paths and required notices. Use “none” where applicable; do not skip the section.

## Verification and rollout

List acceptance/test IDs, required automated/manual evidence, failure behavior, rollback and gate implications. Do not use a blank checklist as proof of a pass.

## Decision record

Record who approved what and when, and link the concrete reviewed commit/PR. Do not fill approval fields on behalf of the maintainer.
