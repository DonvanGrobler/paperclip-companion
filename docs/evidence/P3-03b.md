## Security and licensing

ADR 0020 specifies byte bounds, strict metadata, per-request correlation and
buffer ownership. Rejected/cancelled owned buffers are cleared; successful
ownership is explicitly transferred. No arbitrary error content escapes through
logs or returned diagnostics. No process is spawned and no module imports this
contract into the running app. No new dependency, asset, source copying, external
service, permission or native link input. No distribution-license clearance is
inferred. A parsed reply does not prove its sender, native target or consent.

## Limitations and next step

The decoder alone cannot enforce a process timeout, successful exit, child
ownership, concurrency or kill/reap. Those belong in the next fixed synthetic
parent-child transport slice after merge and verified main. Actual external
window capture still requires native identity and policy/consent validation.
G1, G2 promotion, G3 and OD-02 remain open. Maintainer privacy review required.
